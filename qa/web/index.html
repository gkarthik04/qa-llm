    How it works

    1. RetrieveThe question is matched two ways: dense (MiniLM sentence embeddings, FAISS cosine search) and lexical (BM25). The two rankings are fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, so exact names and numbers are not lost.
    2. Re-rankA cross-encoder (MS-MARCO MiniLM) reads the question and each of the top 20 passages together and reorders them. Its scores become a probability for each passage.
    3. ReadExtractive: DistilBERT, fine-tuned by us on SQuAD, scores every start/end span. With calibrated scoring, each window's logits become probabilities and are multiplied by the passage probability, so the scores are comparable across passages. Generative: Flan-T5 writes the answer from the passages, and its confidence is the probability of the generated sequence.
    4. Cross-checkIn Compare mode both readers answer from the same evidence. When they agree, accuracy is much higher; when they disagree, the UI flags the answer.
    5. Your documentsUploaded PDFs and text files are split into overlapping ~120-word passages and indexed into their own dense + BM25 index, so the same pipeline answers questions about them.
